I'm Vlad Manea, based in Germany, and I help creators turn the attention they already have into a paid offer and their first online client.

I started my own brand in 2023, next to a full-time job. Before that I spent more than a decade in creative and technical work: photography, editing and color grading on one side, quality assurance and software testing on the other. I built my own tools and systems because I needed them, and at some point I noticed they were worth more than my hours. I turned them into digital products, and more than 1,000 photographers have used my Color Grading Manual. I've worked with brands like Adobe, Røde and PGYTech, and I have a degree in psychology, which turned out to be useful for understanding why people buy.

Today I run the Customer Unlock Sprint, a 12-week cohort where creators, coaches and consultants go from "people like my posts" to a clear offer, warm leads, a simple checkout and a first paying client. If you want the short version first, start with the First Client Roadmap.

I write here about the unglamorous parts: how to pick one thing to sell, how to talk to the people already watching you, and how to build something of your own while you still have a 9-to-5 and a life.
